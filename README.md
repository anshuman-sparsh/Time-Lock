# TIME//LOCK

**Time moves when you do. Every action wakes the battlefield.**

TIME//LOCK is a fast browser-based first-person shooter built around one rule: the world nearly freezes when you stop. Enemy movement, incoming fire, and combat pressure return as soon as you move, aim, or shoot.

You are trapped inside a hostile temporal combat simulation. Learn to control its rhythm, survive a sequence of increasingly dangerous rooms, unlock a second weapon, and confront the Warden at the heart of the system.

## The experience

- Freeze the battlefield by standing still.
- Read enemy attacks and plan your next move.
- Dash, jump, strafe, and fire to bring time back to life.
- Fight through seven encounters with automatic room transitions.
- Choose LOW, MEDIUM, or HARD before beginning a campaign.
- Retry the current room without losing campaign progress.
- Defeat the Warden and break out of the simulation.

## Weapons

### Pulse Pistol

A precise, fast sidearm with eight rounds. Its sharp shots reward careful aim and can deal extra damage with headshots.

### Scattergun

A heavier close-range weapon unlocked during the campaign. Its wide blast is strongest up close and creates a larger burst of temporal activity.

## Threats

- **Gunner:** strafes, repositions, and fires clearly visible projectiles.
- **Charger:** warns you before rushing into close-range attacks.
- **Marksman:** maintains distance and commits to powerful precision shots.
- **Burst Drone:** moves through the air and attacks in short bursts.
- **Sentinel:** advances behind a frontal shield; flank it, wait for an opening, or target its head.
- **The Warden:** a three-phase final opponent with projectile spreads, rotating beams, and a vulnerable red core.

## Campaign

The simulation begins with a short temporal calibration and builds through First Contact, Pressure, Sightline, Vertical Threat, Control, and the final Warden encounter. Each room introduces a different mix of movement, cover, attack patterns, and positioning challenges.

After a room is cleared, a **2 → 1 → 0** countdown begins and the next encounter starts automatically.

## Difficulty

- **LOW:** the most forgiving option, with slower and less accurate threats.
- **MEDIUM:** the intended TIME//LOCK experience.
- **HARD:** faster, more accurate, and more aggressive enemies.

Difficulty stays fixed for the current campaign. A new difficulty can be selected when starting again from the main menu.

## Settings

Adjust master volume, effects, ambience, mouse sensitivity, field of view, camera effects, and graphics quality. Sound level presets are available as LOW, MEDIUM, and HIGH.

## About

TIME//LOCK is a compact combat game about restraint as much as speed. Stopping gives you information. Acting creates danger. Winning means choosing the right moment to move.
